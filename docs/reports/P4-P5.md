### Concurrency tests (integration, real DB, separate connections, `Promise.all`)
- 20 users race for the last unit → exactly 1 HELD, 19 SOLD_OUT, stock 0.
- 30 requests for 10 units → exactly 10 HELD.
- One user, 5 parallel reserves of the same type → 1 HELD, the rest LIMIT_REACHED. Also tested with 5 different types: the 2-hold cap holds.
- Same Idempotency-Key ×10 → 1 reservation. Same key from two users → one hold, one CONFLICT.
- Cancel + reject + expire race (live hold and overdue hold) → exactly one transition wins, stock returned once.
- Double check-in → one ARRIVED, the other INVALID_TRANSITION.
- Sweeper vs. citizen cancel, and two parallel sweeps → stock returned exactly once.
- Two users reserving each other's rows (crossed overdue holds) → no deadlock.

## How to demo
**A05 (concurrency):**
1. Open the Finder on a phone: Stroke → Dhanmondi Care General shows ICU **1** left.
2. Terminal: `npm run demo:race -- --url <live> --hospital dhanmondi-care-general --resource ICU_BED --n 20 --set-available 1`.
3. The table shows exactly 1 HELD and 19 SOLD_OUT, final stock 0. The phone's count drops live, and stock is restored afterwards.
4. In the browser, tap "Hold" twice fast: one hold (idempotency key). A second ICU hold gives LIMIT_REACHED. A sold-out hold shows 3 alternatives.
5. Staff console: check in, then try to check in again in a second tab → INVALID_TRANSITION.

**A01 (real time):**
1. Browser A (citizen) holds a resource. Browser B (Finder, signed out) updates in under 0.5 s with no reload.
2. Two staff tabs edit the same row. The second save shows "Another staff member changed this" with Reload / Re-apply.
3. Staff checks in or rejects → the citizen's tracker changes status instantly.
4. DevTools → Offline: the "Reconnecting…" banner shows. Change stock elsewhere, go Online → the page recovers through a snapshot refetch.

## Final fix wave (this session)
- Staff console: a staff member's own save echo no longer shows a false "changed by someone else" hint.
- Signed-out Finder card where every holdable row is sold out now says "Nothing left to hold here right now" instead of a bare "0".
- Socket room cap (20) re-checked right before `join`, so parallel subscribes can't get past it.
- The acceptance test (d) now also accepts the over-TLS case, where Chromium cuts the socket while offline instead of keeping it open. Recovery is still proven by the required snapshot refetches.
